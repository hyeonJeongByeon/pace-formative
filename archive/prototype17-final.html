Before you practice

What is PACE?

A communication framework that trains you, the patient — not your doctor — to get more out of every visit.

Most communication training in healthcare is aimed at clinicians. PACE flips that. Developed by Dr. Donald Cegala at Ohio State University, it trains patients to participate actively in their own medical visits — asking more questions, sharing fuller symptom details, and checking that instructions landed correctly.

It was tested in a randomized trial across 25 family physicians and 150 patients, and later adapted into e-learning and workshop formats. This tool is one more delivery format in that lineage — practice through role-play before it matters in a real appointment.

What the research found
6.9
questions per visit for patients coached directly — vs. 4.5 for those given a written guide alone
3.7
checking / summarizing statements per visit — vs. 1.5 with a written guide alone
Visits did not take longer. The active ingredient wasn't reading about PACE — it was practicing it. (Cegala et al., 2000; Socha McGee & Cegala, 1998)
