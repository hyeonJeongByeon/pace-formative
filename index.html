Getting more out of a medical visit

PACE is a set of four things patients can do during a visit. Take a couple of minutes to read them — then you'll practice in a short role-play.

P
Present detailed information — describe your symptom fully: when it started, how often, how bad, and how it affects your day.
"It started six weeks ago, and it's bad enough that I've been skipping evening plans."
A
Ask questions — when you don't understand a word, a diagnosis, or a plan, ask directly.
"Can you explain what that term means in plain language?"
C
Check understanding — repeat the plan back in your own words to make sure you got it right.
"So just to make sure I have this: I take one pill each morning, and come back in a month?"
E
Express concerns — say what worries you, and speak up if something doesn't sit right or won't work for you.
"I'm worried this doesn't explain the tiredness — it feels different to me."
In the role-play you'll use a made-up scenario we give you — please don't enter your real personal health information anywhere in this tool.
